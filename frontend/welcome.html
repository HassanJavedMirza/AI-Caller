How it works

Four simple steps from overdue invoice to payment confirmed.

Step 01
Invoice goes overdue

The system automatically detects invoices past their due date and flags them.

Step 02
AI places the call

At the scheduled time, the AI calls the client and has a real conversation about the payment.

Step 03

If the client doesn't pick up after retries, an SMS reminder is sent automatically.

Step 04
Admin reviews logs

Admin listens to recordings, reads transcripts, and sees AI summaries for every interaction.
